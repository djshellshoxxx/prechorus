# PreChorus Wiki

PreChorus is a 32-voice swarm and convergence engine for vocals, instruments and hits (JUCE). The sound builds from a
cloud of related voices that become more recognisable and coherent until they meet the original drop or event.
Version 0.0.1 beta. VST3, CLAP and Standalone on Windows; AU on macOS builds.
